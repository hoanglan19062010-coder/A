# Lessons (redo with the actual book pages + the Aoi sheet)

## Head v1 (raster) — hair + eyes SCRAPPED
- Bangs: 8 thin parallel clumps with lines running all the way to the crown = striped comb. Book (Miko p.132, cover): 5-6 BIG swept clumps, edge lines start halfway down, crown has almost no lines.
- Bob ends drawn as tiny saw teeth. Book: bell silhouette, wide at the cheek (hair ~1.24 head wide), tucks in at chin level, a few big tips curling in plus one outward flick.
- Side locks drawn as straight curtains. Book: 2-3 face-framing clumps per side that curve in under the cheekbone.
- Per-clump shadow strip ran full length = stripes. Book: shadow on the underside / lower half; highlight = short streaks in a ring on the crown.
- Eyes 1.75:1 almond. Book/Miko: ~1.25:1, iris fills most of the opening.

## Head v2 — partial scrap (hair details)
- Silhouette line was hidden by side clumps painted later. Rule: side clumps stay inside the bell; the bell outline is drawn LAST.
- Back hair = shapeless dark blobs. Rule: compact, outlined shape between neck and side locks, a few tips.
- Bang tips at equal heights = zigzag fringe. Rule: vary lengths (one long between the eyes), hook the tips in the sweep direction.
- Clump shadow started at a hard cut -> boxy bars. Rule: wedge that grows from nothing.
- Highlight streaks too short/even = rice grains. Rule: soft band + long streaks of varied length, strong on the lit side.
- Shadow colours were raw multipliers (too dark/saturated). Rule: pick the target shadow colour, derive the multiplier.

## Full figure v1 — SCRAPPED parts
- Sleeves 0.29 head wide with vertical outer contours = pillars; with drop shoulders at +-0.75 the cardigan became a box coat. Rule: shoulder slopes from the neck to ~0.58, sleeve 0.24-0.25 wide, tapering, arm angled slightly in toward the thigh.
- Cuffs drawn as tall rectangles = casts. Rule: short rib band (0.14), slightly narrower than the sleeve, sleeve blouses over it.
- Mirrored offsets used +side for "inward" -> stray lines floating outside the arms. Rule: inward = -side for every arm offset; check each offset.
- Ankles 0.10 wide = stilts. Rule (Aoi legs): knee ~0.22, calf ~0.25, ankle ~0.15.
- Front-view loafers too small and round = jelly beans. Rule: shoes ~0.23 wide, flat sole band, visible opening + strap.
- Under-bust shading drawn as rectangles. Rule: crescent = shifted ellipse minus ellipse, softened.
- Skirt flare +-0.66 under a +-0.56 cardigan read as a tutu. Rule: hem ~ +-0.57.

## Full figure v2 — chest lines, hands, shoes SCRAPPED (silhouette, cardigan, legs, skirt passed)
- Under-bust lines meeting in the middle = a "w"/moustache. Rule: soft shadow only, one short tension line.
- Hand = one blob + thumb blob = mitten. Rule: hide the hand in the cuff, show 4 small fingertips + thumb tip.
- Shoe with vertical sides + straight top line = box. Rule: narrow at the ankle, widest at the toe, U-shaped opening, crisp toe-box shine.

## Full figure v3 — arms re-posed
- Arms hung outside the hips with hands floating = stiff mannequin. Both references rest the hands beside the thighs. Rule: elbow near the waist, forearm angles in, cuff follows the forearm slant.
- Outer silhouette had the same weight as interior lines. Book colour pages use a heavier outer contour. Rule: one ring that straddles the outer edge (1px in, ~1.6px out), so it merges with the existing line with no seam.
